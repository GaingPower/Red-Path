WELCOME, ASTRONAUT.

You have been assigned a mission to explore Jezero Crater and investigate Mars' geology.

YOUR MISSION OBJECTIVES

  • Navigate safely from the crater floor to the delta front.
  • Identify and document geological formations.
  • Collect two rock samples:
    • Sample 01: Igneous rock from the Máaz formation.
    • Sample 02: Sedimentary rock from the ancient delta.
  • Monitor environmental conditions and respond to hazard alerts.
  • Record observations and complete all mission tasks.

RED PATH will guide you with augmented-reality (AR) navigation, track your objectives, and alert you to potential dangers, including dunes, unstable terrain, and dust storms.
